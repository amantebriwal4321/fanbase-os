# Demo script

About two minutes. One browser, no account. Start at the site root.

## 0:00 Public hub

Land on the home page. This is Rhea Kapoor's link, the page she would put in a bio. Point at Night Market Club, Test Kitchen, and Guest spots. Mention the live call already on the hub if you scroll, or leave it for the end.

## 0:20 Join and pitch

On Night Market Club, type the name `Amina Joseph` and click Join community.

Click Fill a sample pitch. The title and the note fill in. Read the first line out loud, then click Send pitch.

The page says it was sent. Click Open the creator desk.

## 0:50 The desk

The rules panel is on screen the whole time. Say that the score is house rules, and the model only names topics when a key is set.

The top card is the new Fort Kochi pitch, marked Start here, with the reason and the point chips.

Under Grouped by topic, Night market holds that pitch plus the dosa cart, the map, and Jonah's audio note. Test kitchen and Guest spot are the other piles. Set aside has the cheer and the followers spam.

## 1:25 Accept and post

On the top card, click Accept and post a call. The call is already written. Click Post to Night Market Club.

## 1:40 The call is live

You land back on the hub, on Calls from Rhea. The new call is marked New. The older weeknight bowl call is still there.

## 1:50 Links

The address bar is the live URL. The footer has the repo: github.com/amantebriwal4321/fanbase-os

To put the seeded room back, open Creator desk and click Reset demo.
