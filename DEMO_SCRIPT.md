# Demo script

About two minutes. No signup. Start at the site root.

## 0:00 Open the room

Land on the home page. Point at the line about filter, clusters, and the daily letter. Click Enter Field Notes.

You are in Lena Park's room. No account yet. The list is live seed data: night walks, make kits, and season four pitches. The right side shows today's letter and the clusters.

## 0:20 Show an idea people already joined

Open "Night walk on the freight spur". Show the votes, Priya's comment about printing the map, and her note under Working on this.

## 0:35 Switch to a member

Click Demo member. You are Jordan Hale. Stay on the idea, or go back to the room.

## 0:45 The filter blocks junk

Click Post an idea. Submit this:

Title: `BUY FOLLOWERS NOW`

Body: `BUY FOLLOWERS NOW click here http://bit.ly/x free money for every fan today`

The house filter turns it away and keeps the draft on the form.

## 1:05 Post a real idea

Replace it with:

Title: `Night walk through the covered market`

Body: `Night walk through the covered market after the stalls close. The tile is still warm. We write down three smells and leave by the east gate.`

Submit. The idea is kept. It lands in the Night walk cluster. The page says House filter, unless a model key is set.

## 1:20 Vote, comment, join

Go back to the room and upvote "Episode pitch: the 4am bakery". Open "Make kit: tin can mic". Comment: `I can test a build this week.` Join it with the note: `I will report how it sounds.`

## 1:35 Creator promotes the new walk

Click Demo creator. You are Lena. Open the covered market idea. Pin it, then mark it In progress.

## 1:50 Write today's letter

Open Today's letter. Read the seeded brief. Click Write today's letter. The new letter names the covered market post. If no model key is set, the page says House letter.

Done. To put the original room back, confirm Restore demo at the bottom of the letter page.
